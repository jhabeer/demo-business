Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be home during cleaning?

No. Many clients give access to a trusted contact or the estate security. We’ll confirm the arrangement before the visit.

Do you bring your own equipment and products?

Yes. Our crews arrive with all equipment, colour-coded cloths and eco-friendly products. Special requests are welcome.

Can you handle regular contracts?

Yes. We offer daily, weekly and monthly plans for offices, schools and estates, with a dedicated supervisor.

How fast can you start?

Most home and small-office jobs can be scheduled within 24–48 hours. Larger projects need a short site visit first.
